South Korea Adventure

Exploring Seoul and Busan — a journey through vibrant cities, culture, and cuisine!

Day 1: Seoul Landmarks

Arrived in Seoul and visited the majestic Gyeongbokgung Palace. Wandered through Bukchon Hanok Village, admiring traditional Korean houses.

Day 2: Food & Nightlife

Sampled street food at Myeongdong and enjoyed a night out in Hongdae, famous for its vibrant youth culture and live music.

Day 3: Busan Beaches & Markets

Took the KTX train to Busan. Relaxed at Haeundae Beach and explored Jagalchi Fish Market, tasting fresh seafood and soaking in the coastal vibes.

Travel Tips for South Korea

  • Best time to visit: March-May or September-November
  • Get a T-money card for easy public transport
  • Try Korean BBQ and street food
  • Learn basic Korean phrases for a better experience
