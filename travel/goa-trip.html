Goa Trip

Sun, sand, and the sound of waves — a perfect Goa getaway!

Day 1: Beaches & Sunset

We started our trip at Baga Beach, soaking in the warm sun and enjoying the calm sea breeze. As the day ended, we witnessed one of the most beautiful sunsets at Anjuna Beach.

Day 2: Culture & Food

Explored Old Goa’s historic churches and indulged in mouth-watering seafood. The night ended with music and dancing at a beach shack.

Day 3: Adventure & Shopping

Our last day was packed with water sports at Calangute, followed by shopping for souvenirs at the local flea markets. We wrapped up with a relaxing evening cruise on the Mandovi River.

Goa Location

Travel Tips for Goa

  • Best time to visit: November to February
  • Rent a scooter to explore hidden beaches
  • Try authentic Goan fish curry and bebinca
  • Respect local culture and environment
